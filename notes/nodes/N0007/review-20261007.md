## First unclosed source-semantic step

Wang-v1 (1.8) lists $\sqrt\rho\,u_t$ and $\nabla u_t$, while Definition 1.1 explicitly describes derivatives in the *conservative* equations. One cannot apply a weighted local Poincaré estimate directly to an arbitrary distribution $u_t$: a spatially constant singular temporal derivative has zero spatial gradient. The conventional reading of (1.8) uses one measurable, locally integrable weak-time-derivative function $g=u_t$, whose weighted product and spatial gradient are the listed fields. Neither run found a counterexample in that conventional class, but the source excerpt does not itself construct the representative.

A source-faithful proof must establish or cite that common representative and its spatial slices, without adding $u_t\in L^2_{\rm loc}$ as an extra hypothesis. If the conserved mass is positive, compactness of $\rho([0,T])\subset L^1$ supplies a fixed positive-mass ball. Weighted Poincaré then derives $u_t\in L^2_tL^2_{\rm loc}$ from the displayed weighted and gradient bounds. If the mass is zero, $\rho=P=f_i=0$ and conservative momentum directly gives the weak stress identity.

## Remaining analytic and formal checks

On a bounded ball,
$$
u\in L^\infty_tL^{4/\alpha}_x,\quad
\nabla u\in L^\infty_tH^1_x
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
u\in L^\infty_tH^2_{\rm loc}
   \subset L^\infty_tL^\infty_{\rm loc}.
$$
Continuity then gives
$\rho_t=-u\cdot\nabla\rho-\rho\operatorname{div}u
\in L^\infty_tL^2_{\rm loc}$.
Once the common $u_t$ representative is established, time mollification yields
$\partial_t(\rho u_i)=\rho_tu_i+\rho u_{i,t}$.
The spatial product rule and continuity cancel the extra terms, giving
$f_i=\operatorname{div}V_i$ in spacetime distributions.
A countable $C^1$-dense test family yields one time null set independent of both $i$ and the compactly supported smooth spatial test. The red-team accepted these steps *after* the representative repair. No all-time assertion follows.
